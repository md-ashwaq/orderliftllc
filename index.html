About the company

A second storefront that never needs a dining room.

Order Lift LLC is a kitchen-capacity studio. We sit between your existing restaurant and the apps people already use for dinner. The food still leaves your pass. The brand on the bag is one we build with you, aimed at cravings your dining room was never designed to catch.

  • Menus written for boxes and short rides, using equipment you already own.
  • Item names, photos, and listing copy tested against what nearby diners search after 7 p.m.
  • Orders routed to the tablet your expo already watches, so the line does not learn a new ritual.

Because the kitchen is already built, a second concept can open in weeks. That is the whole point: more covers from the same square footage, without pretending you opened a new address.

Why kitchens call

Three pieces of work. One extra brand on the pass.

We stay out of your dining room service. The work is the digital shelf, the ticket path, and the menu math.

Listing brands

Names, cover photos, and item stories for a delivery-only concept that can soak up unused burner time. Each brand is written to sit beside your current restaurant, not to replace it.

Ticket desk

App orders, catering slips, and your own web cart land in one queue. Modifiers stay readable, late tickets get flagged, and the expo stops juggling four glowing screens.

Menu math

We price what travels, pair sides that share a pan, and retire dishes that look good in a booth and fall apart in a car. The card stays short enough for a Friday rush.
